Title: Urgent Recruitment: Principal Avian Deterrence Engineer (Acoustics Specialist)

Are you a master of the perfect screech? Can you convince a pigeon that the Town Hall is a haunted forest?

The Council is dealing with an unprecedented avian over-population event. We are looking for an elite Acoustics Specialist to deploy high-fidelity bird calls to safely but aggressively relocate our feathered residents to the next borough over.

The Role:

Acoustic Deterrence Strategy: Develop and perform a library of high-impact bird calls. You will be expected to master the "Alarm Call of a Very Angry Raptor" and the "Structural Concern Screech of a Disgruntled Heron."

Vector-Based Displacement: You will map avian nesting clusters and use precision sound-delivery to push populations toward the outskirts of the city.

Infrastructure Audits: Regularly inspect local statues and ledges for nesting activities. If a statue looks like it is starting to become a public pigeon lavatory, your intervention is required immediately.

Technical Requirements:

Expert-level vocal range. A background in operatic singing or professional screeching is highly preferred.

Proficiency in digital sound-blasting hardware and acoustic projection mapping.

Ability to understand "Migratory Vector Logic"—if the birds don't move, you must be able to modulate your pitch to increase the "Terror-to-Decibel" ratio.

The Salary & Perks:

A salary that is vastly higher than what the local school teachers make, but significantly less than what the birds would pay you to stop screeching at them.

A mandatory "Sore Throat" allowance and a lifetime supply of artisanal honey and lemon.

A custom-built, sound-proofed high-seat in the Town Hall tower.
